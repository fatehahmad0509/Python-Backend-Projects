# 💰 Bank Interest Calculator (Python CLI)

This is a simple Python CLI tool that calculates net interest income from a bank deposit, taking into account annual interest rate and withholding tax.

---

## 🚀 Features

- Calculates gross annual interest
- Applies withholding tax deduction
- Shows net annual interest
- Calculates monthly net interest
- Simple command-line interface

---

## 🧠 How It Works

The program takes three inputs:

- Deposit amount (USD)
- Annual interest rate (%)
- Withholding tax rate (%)

Then it calculates:

- Gross annual interest
- Net annual interest after tax
- Monthly net income

---

## 📊 Formula Used

- Gross Interest = Deposit × Interest Rate / 100  
- Net Interest = Gross Interest × (100 - Tax Rate) / 100  
- Monthly Interest = Net Interest / 12  

---
