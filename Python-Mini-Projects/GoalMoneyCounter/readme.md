🪙 Money Counter

A simple Python console application that calculates how many months it will take to reach a savings goal based on your current money and monthly deposits.

🚀 Features
💰 Savings goal calculator
📅 Month-by-month estimation
🧮 Simple and fast console input
⚡ Lightweight Python script (no external libraries)
🧠 How It Works

The program takes:

Current money you own (USD)
Target savings goal (USD)
Monthly deposit amount (USD)

Then it simulates monthly savings until the goal is reached.

📦 Example
Welcome to the Money Counter! Calculate the time left to reach your savings goal

Enter The Money You Have (USD): 1000
Enter Your Goal (USD): 5000
Enter Your Monthly Deposit (USD): 500

Here is the result! You will reach your target amount in 8 months!
